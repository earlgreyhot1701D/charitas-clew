💡 What is Charitas Clew & How to Use It Guide & Scope ▾

Charitas Clew is a zero-judgment paperwork sanctuary that transforms dense legal notices, agency letters, utility disconnections, medical bills, and official paperwork into plain language and clear, actionable steps.

1️⃣ Input Any Document: Paste text or upload a photo/PDF scan of ANY official letter or notice. 2️⃣ Simplify: Click "Simplify Notice" to translate legalese into calm, plain language. 3️⃣ Take Action: Review your key dates, simple next steps, and hand-off speaking script.

📄 Universal Support: Works with eviction warnings, utility disconnects, SNAP/Medicaid renewals, court summonses, hospital/medical bills, tax agency letters, HOA notices, wage garnishments, and all bureaucratic paperwork.

⚖️ Legal Advisement: Educational & self-advocacy tool only — not formal legal advice. Please verify document details with legal aid or a caseworker.
⚠️ SERVICE OFFLINE: This automated tool is currently sunsetted. For immediate housing, legal, or utility assistance, please dial 211 or visit your local Legal Aid Society.
